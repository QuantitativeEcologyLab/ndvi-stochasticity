* Environmental stochasticity is rarely accounted for in spatial ecology
* Estimates of environmental stochasticity depend on the estimated mean
* We present a global, Dynamic Estimate of NDVI Variance: DENVar
* Rasters of DENVar can be downloaded freely from a GitHub repository
* The DENVar rasters should be used with the associated rasters of estimated mean NDVI
